Technology

HALIS is a smart lobby AI system that can revolutionize the receptionist position and approachability. Powered by cutting-edge artificial intelligence, HALIS seamlessly handles visitor check-ins, answers queries, manages access control, and delivers a premium first impression — 24/7, without fatigue.

About HALIS

HALIS was the brainchild of Ariel Zinger in 2025, as he searched for a good business product to create a massive impact on the PropTech industry. Driven by a vision to modernize commercial real estate interactions, HALIS bridges the gap between human hospitality and intelligent automation — setting a new standard for smart buildings.
